How this board prices a token that does not exist

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth factor × maturity factor. The category multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of this snapshot's own already-trading protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the snapshot-wide median (×0.20): a category needs about eight trading peers before its own median outweighs the snapshot's, and a category with no trading peers is priced at the snapshot-wide median itself — so the market's own pricing of live tokens anchors every estimate, and no name is hardcoded. The growth factor adds 30% of the protocol's seven-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+200%; the maturity factor adds up to +30% for four years of being tracked with no token recorded. Where the snapshot reports no seven-day change or no tracking date, that factor is a neutral ×1.00, never a guess. Protocols whose token already trades are listed separately and never ranked. One listing per protocol: where a protocol appears under several versions, the board ranks its largest listing. Wrapped, staked, bridged and restaked products of tokens that already trade — the snapshot's bridge, liquid-staking and restaking categories among them — and exchange balance sheets are set aside with their reasons, because they are not waiting on a launch of their own. Every figure on this page is computed from the snapshot alone.
